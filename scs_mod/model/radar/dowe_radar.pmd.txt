## What This Model Should Look Like

Based on the design preview in `ui-mockups/src/App.tsx`:

- A compact radar detector device
- Dark metallic housing
- Raised ridge at the top
- Glass display in the middle
- 5 buttons at the bottom (MUTE, VOICE, DIM, SENS, PWR)
- Signal strength bars
- Directional arrows (left, front, right)
- Mounting bracket with suction cup

---

## Important: Don't Put Text in the Model

The model should have the SHAPE and COLORS, but NOT the text.

❌ DON'T put in the model:
- Speed numbers (like "65 MPH")
- Frequency numbers (like "34.700 GHz")
- Location text (like "I-5 S / OR")
- Alert text (like "CHP CRUISER")
- Any changing numbers

✅ DO put in the model:
- The physical shape
- The colors (dark housing, red/green/yellow LEDs)
- The button shapes
- The arrow shapes
- The signal bar shapes
- The mounting bracket

The game software will handle the changing text and numbers.
